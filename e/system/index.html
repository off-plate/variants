E, the system

The guideline page for the Off Plate direction E, the bento. The homepage carries 24 widget instances; they collapse into the eleven widget layouts and ten atoms on this page, and nothing else. A layout that appears here once may be filled with any content; a layout missing from here gets added here first and used on the page second.

Provenance. Navy #002B59, teal #1F7A7A and amber #D08C2E are the locked Off Plate palette from BRAND.md; mint, sand and sky are those three raised toward the cream. The mesh gradient uses the same four colours and nothing else.

Rules that fall out of this

Each verdict owns its tint: sky means nothing, sand means automation, mint means AI, and the knob colours match (muted, amber, teal). Amber never carries text on a light ground. Tints never sit on tints; every widget sits on the white sheet.

Type as roles

Sora 700 for headings, Inter 400/500/600 for everything read. Numbers, money and dates take tabular figures via .num. No monospace anywhere in E, by decision. H4 to H6 do not exist; anything below H3 is body or meta.

Banned in E, by decision

Monospace type. Dot separators anywhere, navigation included. Eyebrow labels above headings and cadence labels under them. Translate fade-up reveals. Shadows on widgets. A second dark widget in one row. Tints stacked on tints. A twelfth widget layout that has not been added to this page first.
