E, the system

The guideline page for the Off Plate direction E, the bento. Every token and every component the homepage uses is rendered here once, in its states. If two things do the same job for the user they are one component here, and context differences are modifiers on it. A component missing from this page gets added here first and used on the page second.

Provenance. Navy #002B59, teal #1F7A7A and amber #D08C2E are the locked Off Plate brand palette from BRAND.md; the mint, sand and sky tints are those three colours raised toward the cream so widgets can carry colour without shouting. The mesh gradient is built from the same four brand colours and nothing else.

Colour as roles

A colour is a job. A new widget takes the role that matches its job and has no other choice.

Rules that fall out of this

Each verdict owns its tint: sky means nothing, sand means automation, mint means AI, and the knob colours match (muted, amber, teal). Amber never carries text on a light ground. Tints never sit on tints; every widget sits on the white sheet.

Navigation

The header is a floating island: a white pill that carries the mark, the section links and the button. At the top of the page it rests flush; on scroll it lifts on the page's one shadow and the brand toggle flips from off to on, which is the signature doing the work of a scrolled state. Section links highlight as their section passes. Under 1180px the links move to a drawer of 58px rows that closes on Escape and on link tap and returns focus to the burger; under 620px the button moves into the drawer too.

Banned in E, by decision

Monospace type. Dot separators anywhere, navigation included. Eyebrow labels above headings and cadence labels under them. Translate fade-up reveals. Shadows on widgets. A second dark widget in one row. Tints stacked on tints.
