F, the system

The guideline page for the Off Plate direction F, the magazine layout. Every token and every component the page uses is rendered here, in every state, once. If two things do the same job for the user, they are one component here, and context differences are modifiers. A component missing from this page gets added here first and used on the page second.

Provenance. The amber is the brand toggle taken from the Off Plate logo, darkened to #A66B1B where it carries text so it reads on the warm ground. Ink, ground and paper are the Off Plate cream family pushed one step warmer to sit under black display type. Navy and teal stay reserved for the other directions; F deliberately runs on one accent.

Colour as roles

A colour is a job. A new component takes the role that matches its job and has no other choice.

Rules that fall out of this

One accent, no gradients in F. Amber on ground is decoration only; the moment it carries text it becomes amber-dk. Hairlines are --line on ground and --line-2 inside components, and they are the only borders. Radius is 0 everywhere with no exceptions. Shadows do not exist in F.

Type as roles

Two families, no third. Sora 700 for display, always uppercase, tracked tight. Inter 400/500/600 for everything that is read. Numbers and dates take tabular figures via .num; there is no monospace anywhere in F.

Navigation

Judged as an image, per DESIGN.md. The sticky bar carries three things that work: an amber reading-progress line along its bottom edge, the brand toggle that flips from off to on once you pass the masthead, and the filter rail pinned beneath it. On mobile the links move to a full-screen drawer with 70px rows; the drawer closes on Escape, on link tap, and returns focus to the burger. Nothing else is sticky.

Banned in F, by decision

Monospace type anywhere. Middot separators anywhere, navigation included. Eyebrow labels above headings and cadence labels under them; a heading carries its own meaning or it is rewritten. Border radius. Shadows. A second accent. Fade-up reveals.
